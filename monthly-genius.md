# Mizan Genius of the Month

## Definition

The title is a monthly recognition based on sustained, eligible performance rather than a single exceptional attempt.

## Decision flow

```text
Monthly data
    ↓
Eligibility Gate
    ↓
Integrity checks
    ↓
MGI calculation
    ↓
Tie-break rules
    ↓
Monthly ranking
    ↓
Official recognition
```

## Status

The system should distinguish between:

- Official
- Provisional
- Not eligible

A result should not be promoted as an official Monthly Genius result if the eligibility or integrity checks are incomplete.

## Supporting recognitions

To avoid reducing all excellence to one winner, MIZAN may recognize:

- Accuracy Champion
- Speed Champion
- Consistency Champion
- Rising Genius

These are complementary awards, not substitutes for clear methodology.

## Publication standard

A published result should communicate:

- month
- competition scope
- eligibility status
- MGI
- major component indicators
- recognition category

Do not publish sensitive personal analytics.
